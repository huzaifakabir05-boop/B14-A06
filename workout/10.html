Walking Lunge

Unilateral stepping pattern that builds quads, glutes, and balance under load.

Legs
Equipment
Dumbbells (optional)
Difficulty
Beginner
Sets
3
Reps
10-12/leg
Duration
18 min
Calories
170 kcal
Rating
4.4

Instructions

  1. 1.Step forward and drop the back knee toward the floor.
  2. 2.Keep the front knee stacked over the mid-foot.
  3. 3.Drive through the front heel to the next step.
  4. 4.Stay tall through the torso and control each landing.
