Kettlebell Swing

Explosive hip hinge that builds posterior power, grip, and conditioning in one move.

Full BodyShoulders
Equipment
Kettlebell
Difficulty
Intermediate
Sets
5
Reps
12-15
Duration
16 min
Calories
200 kcal
Rating
4.7

Instructions

  1. 1.Hinge, hike the bell back between the legs, then snap the hips.
  2. 2.Let the bell float to chest height with loose arms.
  3. 3.Brace at the top, then hinge as the bell falls.
  4. 4.Never squat the swing — it is a hinge, not a squat.
